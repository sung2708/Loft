# Loft logo exploration

All five symbols were designed from scratch as monochrome geometric marks. Ratings assess this exploration, not legal or trademark uniqueness.

| Concept | Structure | Uniqueness | Memorability | Favicon | Emotional fit | Warmth | Generic risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Converge | Two independent slabs approaching a shared seam | Medium | High | High | Good | Medium | Medium: butterfly/bow-tie association |
| 02 Orbit | Three open arcs around a common center | Medium | Medium | Low | Good | Medium | High: loading indicator association |
| 03 Echo | A small curve responding to a larger one | Medium | Medium | Medium | Good | Good | Medium: swoosh association |
| 04 Overlap | Two offset rounded fields forming one silhouette | Low | Medium | High | Fair | Medium | High: generic stacked blocks |
| 05 Gather | Three asymmetric forms clustered around one shared opening | High | High | Good | Excellent | High | Low–medium: floral association |

## Finalists

**Gather** is recommended. Its three nearby forms imply distinct presences without depicting people. The small central opening gives the group a shared space. Its irregular rhythm feels more human than a rigid grid, and the silhouette remains legible at 16, 24, 32, 48, and 64 px.

**Converge** is the restrained alternative. It reproduces especially cleanly at small sizes, but its paired symmetry is cooler and has a possible bow-tie reading.

The SVGs are the editable masters. The wordmark uses a portable system sans-serif stack; for production identity use, convert the approved wordmark to outlines after choosing the final font on the target platform.

Colors: black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`, Loft Blue `#0066CC`, dark-mode blue `#2997FF` on `#111318`.
